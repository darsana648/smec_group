      FAQ

      Questions candidates ask us.

      Can I apply to more than one SMEC company?

      Yes. Apply for up to three roles at a time, and we’ll consider you for the best fit across the group.

      I studied at SMEC. Can I apply?

      Absolutely. Many of our trainers and developers are SMEC alumni. Mention your batch in your application.

      Do you support visas for the UAE roles?

      For roles at SMEC Marine Solutions LLC and SMEC Oil & Gas Solutions LLC, visa support is provided as per company policy. Details are shared during the process.

      What does the trainer interview involve?

      A short demo class on a topic you choose, plus a technical discussion. We look for clarity and real-world experience.

      How will my data be used?

      Only to assess your application, in line with India’s DPDP Act. We keep it for 12 months unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
