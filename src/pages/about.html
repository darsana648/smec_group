Twenty-five years of turning skills into careers and ideas into industry.

SMEC began in Kochi in 2001 as an automation engineering company. Today it is a group that trains professionals, builds software and delivers engineering across India, the Middle East, Singapore and the United States.

One idea connects every SMEC company.

Real industry experience makes better training, and better-trained people make better industry.

Our engineers teach, our learners build, and our clients benefit from both. That loop — from learning a skill to running it in the field — is how SMEC has grown from one Kochi office to four countries.

Our structure

One parent company. Specialists underneath.

SMEC Automation Pvt. Ltd. is the parent of the group. Its training arm, SMEClabs, grew into a network of training centres across South India. SMEC Technologies, its IT division at Infopark, combines software training with a working development company.

Outside India, SMEC Marine Solutions LLC in Dubai and SMEC Oil & Gas Solutions LLC in Abu Dhabi deliver engineering for the region’s marine and energy sectors. Offices in Singapore and Orlando support clients and learners around the world.
